## About Me

Hi, I am Mobin Yousefi, an MSc Computer Science graduate focused on AI engineering, software development, and optimization research. My work sits between research-oriented experimentation and production-oriented engineering: I design intelligent applications, backend systems, analytical platforms, AI assistants, and optimization-driven decision-support tools.

My path has moved from computer science fundamentals into metaheuristic optimization, machine learning, backend and full-stack software, and applied AI systems. I am especially interested in turning research ideas into working software that can be tested, improved, deployed, and used.
